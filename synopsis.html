1. Brief Overview of the Project

FORGE & FUEL MATRIX is a unified, high-performance web platform designed to streamline resistance training, biomechanical exercise visualization, precision nutrition planning, and athletic conditioning into an optimized Single Page Application (SPA).

Purpose: Modern fitness enthusiasts often juggle 3 to 4 fragmented apps (one for calorie counting, one for workout tracking, separate video guides, and notes). FORGE & FUEL solves this by coupling scientific metabolic equations with interactive 2D canvas animations, AI-assisted form coaching, natural language logging, and offline local-first storage.

Major Functions:

  • Metabolic Engine & Macro Calculator: Real-time calculation of Mifflin-St Jeor BMR, dynamic TDEE modifiers, and macro splits (Protein, Carbs, Fats) across Cut, Bulk, and Recomposition.
  • Workout Builder & Fullscreen Gym Mode: Drag-and-drop circuit routine creation with millisecond timer, rest countdowns, and Web Audio API synthesized cues.
  • PR Engine & 84-Day Activity Heatmap: Epley 1RM formula calculations, automatic personal record celebrations, and weighted Consistency Score index (0–100).
  • AI Coach & NLP Workout Logger: Biomechanical movement advice, circadian recovery predictions, and regex NLP parsing of unstructured lifting notes.
  • Community Squads & Canvas Share Cards: Activity feed, mutual kudos, squad volume leaderboards, and 800×1000 story cards exported via Web Share API or PNG.
  • Wearables Sync & Data Portability: Multi-device biometric sync (Apple Health, Google Fit, Garmin, Fitbit), Spotify BPM matcher, and CSV/.ICS exports.

Target Users: University students, fitness enthusiasts, competitive lifters, and personal trainers seeking a high-performance, private, and subscription-free cockpit.

Expected Contribution: A zero-latency, local-first PWA operating completely offline in modern web browsers, eliminating paid subscriptions while maintaining absolute user data sovereignty.

2. Background of the Project

Maintaining physical health is essential, yet modern fitness apps suffer from severe ecosystem fragmentation and aggressive monetization. Commercial applications lock basic features like macro counting or workout logging behind monthly subscriptions ($15–$30/month), inject disruptive advertisements, and stall over spotty gym connectivity. FORGE & FUEL MATRIX provides an open, science-grounded single-page web platform that works locally with zero server dependence or financial barriers.

4. Problem Statement

Fitness beginners and athletes fail to sustain progress due to three major challenges: (1) nutritional ambiguity without personalized caloric/protein prescriptions, (2) elevated injury risk caused by poor form visualization and excessive fatigue, and (3) app fatigue caused by paid subscriptions, ads, and network latency in gym basements.

Proposed Solution: Develop FORGE & FUEL MATRIX, an all-in-one, local-first web application that operates instantaneously, integrates rigorous metabolic science (Mifflin-St Jeor BMR, dynamic TDEE modifiers), provides interactive 2D canvas biomechanical animations, and offers complete offline-capable logging of meals, workout sets, and hydration levels without subscription barriers.

5. Objectives and Scope of the Project

5.1 Objectives

  • O1: Implement the Mifflin-St Jeor equation to compute Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) with dynamic macro splits for Cut, Bulk, and Recomposition.
  • O2: Build a Full-Screen Gym Mode with millisecond-precision timers, rest interval chime countdowns via Web Audio API, and automated Epley 1RM PR detection.
  • O3: Develop procedural HTML5 Canvas 2D motion visualizers for compound movement kinematics and 800×1000 high-res social workout share cards.
  • O4: Build a regex-powered client-side Natural Language Processing (NLP) parser to extract exercises, sets, reps, and weights from freeform session notes.
  • O5: Formulate an algorithmic Consistency Score index (0–100) and an 84-day activity heatmap tracking training adherence and hydration thresholds.
  • O6: Integrate an athlete social network with squad creation, weekly volume leaderboards, community feed kudos, and peer cheer interactions.
  • O7: Connect health wearables (Apple Health, Google Fit, Garmin, Fitbit) and acoustic Spotify BPM workout matching with complete CSV and RFC 5545 `.ics` data portability.

5.2 Scope

What the Project Covers: Unified biometric profiling, metabolic engines, circuit routine builder, guided gym mode, 2D kinematic animations, daily diet/water tracking, 84-day activity heatmap, consistency scoring, challenge streaks & badges, social squad leaderboards, AI form coaching & regex NLP parsing, wearable biometric sync, Spotify workout matching, and CSV/.ICS calendar export.
Exclusions (Future Scope): Computer vision camera tracking for automated rep-counting and server-side multi-user relational DB syncing will be explored in future cloud editions.

6. Methodology / Implementation Plan

The project follows an Iterative Agile Methodology across 5 distinct phases: (1) Core Tools & Gym Mode, (2) Insights, Consistency Scoring & Activity Matrix, (3) Social Network & Share Cards, (4) AI Coaching & NLP Parser, and (5) Health Wearables & Data Portability. Each iteration incorporates user testing, automated unit tests, and performance validation.

7. Expected Outcome

The project delivers an advanced, production-grade web application running completely in modern web browsers:

  • Immediate Metabolic Prescriptions: Eliminates guesswork with instant Mifflin-St Jeor BMR, TDEE, and macro splits.
  • Biomechanical Safety & AI Advice: 2D kinetic canvas form visualizers, joint protection swaps, and chronobiology recovery windows.
  • Rapid Unstructured Logging: Client-side regex NLP parser converting natural lifting notes into structured volume logs in seconds.
  • Social Accountability: Squad leaderboards, mutual cheers, kudos reactions, and 800×1000 story export cards.
  • Total Data Portability & Zero Subscriptions: Operates offline via PWA, synchronizes wearable metrics, and exports CSV/.ICS files freely.
